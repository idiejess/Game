# Character Bible — Halfway Lock

Twelve major recurring characters, eight supporting characters, and a small set of non-character sources. Machine-readable versions live in `content/characters/*.json` and are validated by `schemas/character.schema.json`.

Design rules: nobody is simply good or evil; every disagreement has a reason a reasonable person could hold; each voice must be identifiable with the name hidden; humor is distributed unevenly and on purpose.

---

## Major characters

### 1. Odile Vosk — Summit Auditor (`vosk`) — Company
- **Silhouette**: tall, narrow, grey travelling coat buttoned to the throat, a document case held flat like a tray. Expressions: `neutral`, `measuring`, `tired`, `rare_warmth`.
- **Speech rhythm**: short declaratives; never a question she does not know the answer to; pauses before names.
- **Vocabulary**: "irregular", "noted", "the works", "for the record", "you will find".
- **Prohibited**: slang, exclamation, apology, "I think".
- **Public motivation**: keep the summit accounts and the Keeper honest.
- **Private motivation**: keep the Marrow Cut secret because she believes disclosure ends the canal, the town and the peace between the shores.
- **Fear**: that she is the last person who knows, and that she is wrong.
- **Contradiction**: an auditor whose whole job is concealing the largest fraud on the canal.
- **Secret**: has read Marrow's letters; personally filed Dray as "resigned".
- **Relationship with player**: watcher, occasional protector, potential ally in TE2 or the executioner in "The Promotion".
- **Relationships**: Pell (contempt for his sincerity, protects him from it); Quenn (fear she will find the survey); Mirren (guilt); Hale (they share the chapel's secret without ever saying so).
- **Across runs**: softens toward Keepers who survived long; in run 3+ she may say "the last one asked that too."
- **Arcs**: MA03 (The Auditor's Interest), MA09 (The Vosk File), MA12 (Last Water). Relationship cards ×10.

### 2. Gideon Pell — Superintendent (`pell`) — Company
- **Silhouette**: broad, waistcoat straining, a sash of Company green, a brass speaking trumpet he does not need. Expressions: `beaming`, `neutral`, `wounded`, `stern`.
- **Speech rhythm**: long, rolling, three adjectives where one would do; addresses the player as "Keeper!" as if announcing them.
- **Vocabulary**: "magnificent", "the Meridian spirit", "throughput", "our little difficulty".
- **Prohibited**: profanity, doubt about the Company, admitting fatigue.
- **Public motivation**: make the Jubilee glorious and the traffic figures a record.
- **Private motivation**: be remembered; a Board seat; he has never been to Ardent.
- **Fear**: irrelevance; being laughed at by Sorrel newspapers.
- **Contradiction**: sincerely loves the canal and the town and is the cause of half its floods.
- **Secret**: has been reviving the Clearance Order draft "as a contingency".
- **Relationship with player**: employer and cheerleader; disappointed father when Company falls.
- **Relationships**: Vosk (thinks she is his subordinate); Ilse (long feud over the brewery rent); Hesse (obsessed with his reviews); Crane (rivals in pomposity).
- **Across runs**: greets each new Keeper with the same speech, adjusting the number of "previous unfortunate events".
- **Arcs**: MA01 (The Jubilee), MA04 (Throughput), MA10 (The Clearance). Relationship ×10.

### 3. Tamsin Quenn — Company Engineer (`quenn`) — Company
- **Silhouette**: small, ink-stained cuffs, an oversized folio of drawings, hair escaping a cap. Expressions: `worried`, `absorbed`, `neutral`, `resolved`.
- **Speech rhythm**: fast, self-interrupting, numbers first; apologizes for facts.
- **Vocabulary**: "the figures", "sorry", "it doesn't close", "roughly", "the aqueduct".
- **Prohibited**: "magnificent", metaphors, threats.
- **Public motivation**: keep the gates and paddles working with no budget.
- **Private motivation**: make the reservoir sums balance; they never do.
- **Fear**: being right.
- **Contradiction**: the most honest person on the summit, who keeps her findings in a drawer.
- **Secret**: has a copy of Marrow's Y6 survey she has not dared read fully.
- **Relationship with player**: the ally who needs protecting; can be pushed to publish.
- **Relationships**: Vosk (terror); Pell (exasperation, fondness); Tamm (friendship, shared tools); Mirren (mentor to apprentice).
- **Across runs**: if a prior Keeper protected her, she begins the next run slightly braver (persistent `quenn_courage` unlock).
- **Arcs**: MA01, MA05 (The Reservoir Sums), MA12. Relationship ×10.

### 4. Ilse Harrow — Innkeeper and Town Reeve (`ilse`) — Town
- **Silhouette**: solid, sleeves rolled, apron over a reeve's chain she rarely wears, a tally slate. Expressions: `neutral`, `wry`, `angry`, `grieving`.
- **Speech rhythm**: plain, one clause, then the price. Calls the player "Keeper" without warmth until earned, then by no title at all.
- **Vocabulary**: "cellar", "the moot", "what it costs", "your Company".
- **Prohibited**: flattery, "irregular", religious language.
- **Public motivation**: dry cellars, fair rent, the town's work.
- **Private motivation**: her son drowned in the Y44 riot's aftermath; she wants the town never to rise again, and never to be pushed again.
- **Fear**: the town burning its own bridges.
- **Contradiction**: the moderate who runs the town's only illegal water-sale.
- **Secret**: the brewery well is fed by a crack from the pound; the ale is Company water.
- **Relationship with player**: gatekeeper of the town's regard.
- **Relationships**: Pell (rent war); Bram (old respect, current friction over moorings); Cole (rival); Stroud (friend).
- **Across runs**: remembers each previous Keeper by one act.
- **Arcs**: MA08 (High Water), MA10, minor arcs at the inn. Relationship ×10.

### 5. Mirren Dray — Lock Hand, daughter of the vanished Keeper (`mirren`) — Town / Company
- **Silhouette**: young, wiry, oilskin too large (her father's), a windlass over the shoulder. Expressions: `guarded`, `neutral`, `hopeful`, `broken`.
- **Speech rhythm**: clipped; refuses the last word of a sentence when it hurts; direct questions.
- **Vocabulary**: "he", "the study", "did you", "the paddles".
- **Prohibited**: "father" (says "he" until MA11), jokes about the Company.
- **Public motivation**: keep her post and her father's house.
- **Private motivation**: find out where Ansel Dray went.
- **Fear**: that he left on purpose.
- **Contradiction**: works every day for the Company she suspects killed him.
- **Secret**: has his ledger, in cipher, and has told no one.
- **Relationship with player**: you live in her house and sit in his chair.
- **Relationships**: Quenn (apprentice); Vosk (hostility, unaware of Vosk's guilt); Wren (uneasy friendship); Tobin (the only one who tells her stories about her father).
- **Across runs**: in run 2+ she may distrust new Keepers who "also won't last".
- **Arcs**: MA02 (The Study), MA11 (Into the Cut), MA12. Relationship ×10.

### 6. Bram Holloway — Holder of the Reed Reach (`bram`) — Hullfolk
- **Silhouette**: old, immense, seated always on a bollard or gunwale, a coat of stitched sailcloth, a pipe unlit. Expressions: `neutral`, `patient`, `stern`, `mourning`.
- **Speech rhythm**: slow, plural ("we hold", "we remember"), one proverb per meeting, never a threat, never a plea.
- **Vocabulary**: "holding", "the remembered", "water debt", "the river".
- **Prohibited**: "please", "Company" (says "the charter men"), hurry.
- **Public motivation**: keep the holdings; be left alone.
- **Private motivation**: recover the Hullfolk's name; he believes the Company stole the river on purpose and wants it said aloud.
- **Fear**: that the young will forget, or that they will burn something.
- **Contradiction**: a man of peace whose people are the only ones who could sabotage the canal, and who quietly decides each year not to.
- **Secret**: knows where the Cut's outfall is; has never gone in.
- **Relationship with player**: judge of whether this Keeper is "a gate or a wall".
- **Relationships**: Wren (grandfather-in-all-but-blood, despairs of her); Petronel (his oldest friend, sings what he will not say); Ilse (respect, mooring feud); Solas (two old soldiers of different wars).
- **Across runs**: if a previous Keeper honored the remembered, later runs open with a Hullfolk gift.
- **Arcs**: MA06 (The Water Debt), MA10, MA12. Relationship ×10.

### 7. Wren Ashby — Hullfolk runner and smuggler (`wren`) — Hullfolk
- **Silhouette**: lean, barefoot on gunwales, patched jacket with too many pockets, a coil of line. Expressions: `grinning`, `neutral`, `caught`, `serious`.
- **Speech rhythm**: quick, deflecting, answers questions with offers; funny by understatement.
- **Vocabulary**: "cargo" (for anything), "a favor", "the old man", "you didn't see".
- **Prohibited**: proverbs, "we" (says "I" — the break with Bram's plural is deliberate), formal address.
- **Public motivation**: money and passage.
- **Private motivation**: get the Hullfolk something the town cannot take: coin, leverage, land.
- **Fear**: ending like Bram, sitting on a bollard remembering.
- **Contradiction**: the least sentimental Hullfolk is the one who learns the whole Song.
- **Secret**: runs rifles for Sorrel through the reeds; has stopped once and may again.
- **Relationship with player**: tempter, informant, friend if you let a few things pass.
- **Relationships**: Bram (love, defiance); Petronel (grandmother); Solas (buyer, and the man she fears); Mirren (one honest friendship).
- **Across runs**: if a previous Keeper jailed her, she is absent for early watches of the next run.
- **Arcs**: MA06, MA07 (The Rifle Road), MA12. Relationship ×10.

### 8. Matthias Crane — Aldmere Customs Inspector (`crane`) — Aldmere
- **Silhouette**: precise, black coat, white stock, spectacles, a bound statute book under the arm like a shield. Expressions: `neutral`, `disapproving`, `delighted_by_a_clause`, `alarmed`.
- **Speech rhythm**: complete sentences with citations; deadpan; humor entirely accidental.
- **Vocabulary**: "the Compact", "Schedule Four", "duly", "I note".
- **Prohibited**: guesses, feelings, slang, "roughly".
- **Public motivation**: enforce the Compact to the letter.
- **Private motivation**: prove Aldmere could run the canal; a report he has been writing for six years.
- **Fear**: disorder; also, being liked, which confuses him.
- **Contradiction**: the rule-bound official who becomes the true ending's most useful witness precisely because he will not lie.
- **Secret**: has been counting the water himself; his figures match Quenn's.
- **Relationship with player**: counterweight; ally if you are consistent.
- **Relationships**: Vane (professional loathing that is nearly fondness); Solas (regards the escort company as an armed illegality); Pell (rival pomp); Quenn (admires her precision).
- **Across runs**: files a report on each Keeper; run 3+ he quotes the previous.
- **Arcs**: MA04, MA07, MA09. Relationship ×10.

### 9. Nessa Vane — Sorrel Freight Agent (`vane`) — Sorrel
- **Silhouette**: sharp, travelling suit, a rate card in one hand and a pocket watch in the other. Expressions: `brisk`, `neutral`, `amused`, `furious`.
- **Speech rhythm**: fast, numbers and deadlines; interrupts; ends with a deal.
- **Vocabulary**: "delay costs", "my principals", "by the next watch", "we can fix that".
- **Prohibited**: statutes, prayers, "magnificent", "we hold".
- **Public motivation**: coal east, iron east, faster.
- **Private motivation**: buy the canal's Sorrel half for a consortium when the Company weakens.
- **Fear**: that the canal is dying and her contracts with it.
- **Contradiction**: the person who most wants the Company gone is the one who most needs the canal to be a lie.
- **Secret**: her consortium is financed by the same people who buy Wren's rifles.
- **Relationship with player**: dealmaker; can bail out your Coffers, at a price.
- **Relationships**: Crane (the loathing); Solas (employer of his company); Hesse (feeds him stories); Pell (flatters him).
- **Across runs**: her offers scale with run number ("the last Keeper took the deal").
- **Arcs**: MA04, MA07, MA09. Relationship ×10.

### 10. Captain Ferrant Solas — Sorrel Escort Company (`solas`) — Sorrel (private militia)
- **Silhouette**: worn grey uniform without insignia, one arm stiff, a sabre he never draws, a soldier's slouch. Expressions: `neutral`, `weary`, `hard`, `almost_smiling`.
- **Speech rhythm**: quiet, economical, soldier's understatement, one dry observation per card.
- **Vocabulary**: "my people", "the convoy", "orders", "it won't come to that".
- **Prohibited**: boasts, politics, "magnificent", statute numbers.
- **Public motivation**: get his convoys through; pay his men.
- **Private motivation**: never fight another war; he served in the Y58 near-war and lost the arm to friendly fire.
- **Fear**: being ordered to fire on the town or the Hullfolk.
- **Contradiction**: an armed man who is the summit's most reliable pacifist.
- **Secret**: knows the rifles are for a Sorrel coup faction, not defence.
- **Relationship with player**: the one who will hold the line for you if you have earned it.
- **Relationships**: Bram (mutual regard); Wren (buys her rifles, hates that he does); Vane (paymaster); Crane (mutual suspicion).
- **Across runs**: in run 2+ may mention a Keeper who "asked me to do something I wouldn't."
- **Arcs**: MA07, MA10, MA12. Relationship ×10.

### 11. Sister Hale Ambrey — Chaplain of the Still Water (`hale`) — Concordance
- **Silhouette**: grey robe with a blue hem, a lamp on a chain, hands always occupied with a cloth or a cup. Expressions: `serene`, `neutral`, `troubled`, `unveiled`.
- **Speech rhythm**: gentle, sideways, blesses rather than answers; never says "no".
- **Vocabulary**: "the gift", "still", "tend", "we do not ask the water".
- **Prohibited**: numbers, "irregular", "cargo", accusation.
- **Public motivation**: bless the vessels; keep the Lamp Vigil; feed the poor at the gate.
- **Private motivation**: protect the founding charter; she has read Marrow's letters and has decided the gift is still a gift.
- **Fear**: the spring being explained.
- **Contradiction**: the kindest person on the summit keeps the cruelest secret out of kindness.
- **Secret**: the chapel undercroft holds Marrow's letters and the Concordance's founding donation record.
- **Relationship with player**: comfort, mercy, and the last door before the truth.
- **Relationships**: Vosk (unspoken pact); Bram (she feeds his people, he does not attend); Stroud (medicine vs prayer, friendly); Cole (the chapel's landlady).
- **Across runs**: if a previous Keeper died at the Breach, she holds a vigil card in the next run.
- **Arcs**: MA08 (High Water, chapel thread and the Letters), MA11 (Into the Cut), MA12 (Last Water). Relationship ×10.

### 12. Fennick Orr — Telegraph Clerk (`fennick`) — Town / Company
- **Silhouette**: gangly, eyeshade, ink on his nose, a fan of telegram slips. Expressions: `eager`, `neutral`, `sheepish`, `frightened`.
- **Speech rhythm**: breathless, parenthetical, tells you the thing he should not before the thing he should.
- **Vocabulary**: "you'll never guess", "in strictest confidence", "the wire", "I shouldn't".
- **Prohibited**: discretion, proverbs, threats.
- **Public motivation**: run the wire and be liked.
- **Private motivation**: be important to someone; he sells copies of telegrams to Hesse and to Vane.
- **Fear**: Vosk.
- **Contradiction**: the town's biggest leak is the only person who keeps the player's secrets when it counts.
- **Secret**: he transmitted Dray's last wire and kept a copy.
- **Relationship with player**: information source and liability.
- **Relationships**: Hesse (customer); Vane (customer); Vosk (terror); Mirren (unrequited, hopeless, never stated).
- **Across runs**: remembers every previous Keeper's telegram habits.
- **Arcs**: MA02, MA03, MA09. Relationship ×10.

## Non-character sources
Used as `speaker` for situations without a named speaker. Each has a single generic card illustration.
- `src_gate`: a vessel at the gate; the lock hands report.
- `src_telegram`: a telegram slip.
- `src_weather`: weather and water.
- `src_ledger`: the Keeper's own ledger (reflection, memory, metaprogression).
- `src_stranger`: an unnamed petitioner (procedural minor roles: a bargee, a pilgrim, a refugee family, a drover).

## Voice test
With names hidden a reader should distinguish: statute-citing deadpan (Crane), three adjectives and "Keeper!" (Pell), numbers-then-sorry (Quenn), plural proverbs (Bram), "cargo" and "you didn't see" (Wren), price at the end (Ilse), blessings instead of answers (Hale), soldier's understatement (Solas), deadlines and deals (Vane), clipped questions and "he" (Mirren), "you'll never guess" (Fennick), flat "noted" (Vosk).
