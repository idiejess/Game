# Audio Bible — Halfway Lock

Status: no audio-generation tool is available in the sandbox. Shipped audio consists of short procedurally synthesized placeholder cues (sine/noise, generated by `tools/build_placeholders.py`) and silence for music. All are labeled `placeholder` in `assets/audio_manifest.csv`. Nothing here is final.

## Palette
Small ensemble: fiddle, viola, plucked bass, harmonium, a single tubular bell, hand percussion on wood and iron. Field-style textures: dripping, gear clicks, rope creak, distant tow horse, wind at altitude.

## Motifs
- **The Pound** (title/ambient): a slow harmonium chord that breathes on a 6-second cycle. Its lowest voice is filtered out as Water falls (implemented as a low-pass tied to Water).
- **The River** (Hullfolk/true ending): a single fiddle melody in a minor mode with a raised sixth; first heard as fragments in Petronel's cards.
- **The Charter** (Company): bright plucked bass and bell, rhythmic, slightly too regular.
- **Aldmere**: viola, stately. **Sorrel**: iron percussion, quick. **Concordance**: bell and held harmonium fifth.
