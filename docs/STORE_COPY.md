## Short description (≤ 300 characters; itch.io "short description", app-store subtitle field)
A narrative decision game. Swipe to decide who passes the summit lock, keep the water high enough to float a boat and low enough not to drown the town, and stay in post long enough to learn why the water is falling. 1,000 hand-written situations, 40 endings.

## Long description
At the summit of the Meridian Canal sits Lock No. 9, called Halfway: the highest gate on the only water road across the continent. You are its new Keeper.

Grain barges, smugglers, soldiers, pilgrims, auditors and the boat-clans who live on the water all arrive at your gates, and each wants something that someone else will pay for. Drag each card left or right to decide who passes, who waits, who pays and who is believed. Keep the summit water high enough to float a boat and low enough not to drown the town below. Keep your employer satisfied and the townsfolk fed. Stay in post long enough to notice what every Keeper before you noticed too late: the water is falling, and nobody will say where it comes from.

- **One thousand hand-written situations** across twelve major and thirty minor storylines.
- **Five resources that fail at both ends.** A full pound floods the low street; an empty one strands the fleet. There is no "max everything" play.
- **A clock that is the mystery.** The water falls a little every watch. Finding out why is the game.
- **Twenty characters you learn to recognise by voice** — a Hullfolk elder, a Company auditor, a brewer with a spring that tastes of iron, a telegraph clerk who reads everything twice.
- **A Keeper's Ledger that outlives you.** Each run is a new Keeper; discoveries carry forward and change which endings are possible. Three true endings wait for the player who reads the letters in order.
- **Short sessions, save anywhere, fully offline.** No accounts, no analytics, no purchases.
- **Accessibility built in:** text scaling, high contrast, reduced motion, subtitles for audio cues, keyboard and button play, no timers.
