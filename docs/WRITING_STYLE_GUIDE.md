# Writing Style Guide — Halfway Lock

Enforced partly by `tools/analyze_dialogue.py`. Rules marked (auto) are checked automatically.

## Length (auto)
- Situation text: 6–32 words. Documented exceptions: `end_*` cards may run to 60.
- Choice labels: 1–8 words.
- Outcome text (optional): ≤ 24 words.

## Structure
- One dilemma per card. If a card needs "and also", it is two cards.
- The two choices must express different **priorities, risks or values**, never good vs evil.
- ≥ 70% of ordinary cards involve a tradeoff (at least one resource up and one down, or a resource vs a relationship/flag).
- ≤ 20% of ordinary cards have purely positive or purely negative immediate effects.
- ≥ 25% of the library creates or resolves a delayed consequence (`delayed_effects`, `followups`, or resolves a flag set elsewhere).
- ≥ 30% reacts to state (has a prerequisite beyond category defaults).
- Choices with identical effects are invalid (auto).

## Voice
Each major character has rhythm, vocabulary and prohibited phrases in `docs/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`; the analyzer flags prohibited phrases per speaker (auto). Names hidden, the voice must still identify the speaker.

Speaker attribution: the `speaker` field is who is talking. Third-person situation text is used only for `src_*` sources.

## Tone
Dry, humane, observant. Humor by situation, personality, contrast and consequence. No jokes in: death cards, refugee cards, the Cut, true endings, Petronel's song cards.

## Banned (auto)
- Phrases: "the air is thick", "little did", "a chill ran", "you can't help but", "in a world where", "delve", "tapestry", "testament to", "palpable", "unbeknownst".
- Modern slang: "okay", "guys", "cool", "yeah", "hey", "bro", "whatever", "no way".
- Game mechanics in dialogue: "resource", "flag", "stat", "points", "level up", "unlock" (except in `src_ledger` metacards, which are diegetic reflections and may say "the Ledger").
- Repeated openers: no more than 3% of cards may begin with the same first two words (auto).
- Rhetorical question density: ≤ 1 question mark per situation text unless the speaker is Mirren or Fennick (auto, warning).
- Punctuation: ≤ 1 ellipsis or em dash per card; ≤ 1 exclamation per card, and Vosk/Bram/Solas/Crane never exclaim (auto).

## Mechanical transparency
Do not reveal exact numbers in text. Choice labels may hint at direction ("Charge double", "Wave them through"). The UI shows arrows; exact numbers appear in the accessibility option.

## Naming and setting
No real nations, religions, brands or people. Terminology from `docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md` §10. The player is "Keeper".

## Localization
All text lives in the card JSON under `text`, `left.label`, `right.label`, `left.outcome`, `right.outcome`. The loader generates localization keys (`card.<id>.text` etc.) and `content/localization/en.csv` is generated by `tools/generate_reports.py --localization`, so authors write once.

## Reference card
```
speaker: crane   expression: disapproving
text: "Schedule Four requires both seals on arms. The Sorrel seal is here. Mine is not, and will not be."
left:  "Hold the crate"        → traffic -6, company +4, crane +6, vane -6, flag arms_held
right: "One seal will serve"   → traffic +5, crane -8, vane +6, sorrel +4, flag arms_passed
```
