# Art Bible — Halfway Lock

Status: no image-generation tool is available in the development sandbox. All shipped images are labeled procedural placeholders that preserve final filenames and dimensions (`assets/art_manifest.csv`). This document defines the target style for the eventual asset pass.

## Visual thesis
A Company file at altitude. Each card is a document: an inked portrait pinned to chalk-pale paper, over a horizon of dark summit water whose line is the Water resource itself. Brass, wet slate, lamp amber. Everything is slightly too tidy, the way institutions like things, and the water underneath is not.

## Shape language
Rounded rectangles with one square corner (the "docket" corner) for cards and panels. Characters are built from a few flat shapes with one strong silhouette feature (Pell's sash, Vosk's case, Bram's sailcloth mass). Faction accents are a single colored element per portrait, never a whole costume.

## Line and edge
Uniform ink line, medium weight, slightly rough as if printed. Two-tone cel shading; no gradients on characters. Backgrounds may use soft gradients (water, lamplight).

## Lighting
Single warm lamp source from the lower left on portraits, cool ambient from the water. Night is the default; day cards use a paler chalk.

## Texture
Paper grain on cards (subtle, ≤ 6% opacity). Water has a slow horizontal ripple. No photographic textures.

## Portrait framing and camera
Bust framing, chest up, straight-on or slight three-quarter, eye level. Portrait area 512×640 with head center at 42% height so expressions read on a phone. Transparent background.

## Proportions
Slightly elongated (7.5 heads) for adults; hands drawn deliberately and large because characters hold their identifying objects.

## Faction visual language
Company: symmetry, green, brass fittings. Town: brick, cloth, worn wood. Hullfolk: stitched sailcloth, ochre, rope, asymmetry. Aldmere: black and white, spectacles, paper. Sorrel: rust, iron, watches. Concordance: grey-blue, lamps, cloth.

## Expression system
Major: 4 expressions (see Character Bible). Supporting: 2. Expressions change only the face and hands; silhouette and accent remain identical so swaps are cheap and consistent. Special portraits: Mirren `broken` (MA11 reveal), Vosk `rare_warmth` (TE3), Hale `unveiled` (letters reveal), Bram `mourning` (Clearance).

## UI surfaces
Dark Wet Slate panels, Chalk text, Amber focus. Buttons are dockets (one square corner). Meters are horizontal brass gauges with a needle and a numeric readout in exact mode.

## Resource icons (64×64, monochrome, also shape-distinct)
Water: wave with level tick. Traffic: two opposed arrows. Coffers: strapped chest. Company: sealed charter scroll. Town: lamp on post. Icons differ by shape, not only color; critical state adds a cracked outline.

## Faction icons (64×64)
Company gear-and-wave; Town lamp; Hullfolk reed knot; Aldmere sheaf; Sorrel hammer; Concordance still-water circle.

## Background treatment
Primary: the pound at night from the Keeper's window, water line at the card's midline. Crisis variants: Flood (water high, amber reflections), Dry (exposed sills, cracked mud), Frost (white line), Fire (rust glow), Riot (lamps in the town), Cut (black tunnel, one lamp).

## Card treatment
Front: chalk paper, speaker nameplate top, portrait, text block, two edge tabs that light up when dragging toward them. Back: Wet Slate with the Company gear-and-wave in Charter Green.

## Animation principles
Drag rotation ±12° at threshold; ease-out snap-back 180 ms; commit throw 220 ms; new card rises from the water line. Reduced-motion mode replaces motion with 120 ms fades.

## Safe areas and mobile readability
Portrait 1080×1920 design; all interactive elements within 5% margins; text ≥ 18 sp at default; 44 dp minimum tap targets; landscape and desktop letterbox the card column to a max width of 640 px.

## Prohibited
Photoreal rendering; gradients on characters; medieval iconography (crowns, thrones, heraldry); imitation of any specific existing card game's composition; prompts naming living artists; text baked into images.

## Placeholder policy
Procedural placeholders are generated by `tools/build_placeholders.py`: correct dimensions, faction accent color, silhouette hint, and a clearly printed "PLACEHOLDER" label and asset ID. They are never marked approved in the manifest.
